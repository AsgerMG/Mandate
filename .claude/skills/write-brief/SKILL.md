# Write the brief

The brief is the last stage of the consultation arc: the conversation, wrapped up
for a reader who was not in it (the founder's board, an investor, a co-founder).

1. **Re-read the conversation.** Use what the founder told you and decided, in their
   words where it matters; never re-open what was settled. Where a fact changed during
   the conversation, the brief carries the latest one.
2. **Write it in this order**, plain words, short sentences, no em dashes, about one
   page plus sources:
   1. **The read** (two or three sentences): the company, what it wants, the deciding
      fact.
   2. **The recommendation**: the best way in and why it has the best chance.
   3. **The options we weighed**: one line each, with why it is lead, second, parked
      or shut.
   4. **The plan**: this week, this month, this quarter, later; what must happen first;
      what not to do yet.
   5. **Who**: per route, the institution, the unit, P1 and P2 by function, how to
      reach them.
   6. **What to expect**: the steps of the lead route, what each needs to pass, timing,
      odds.
   7. **Risks and flags**, each with its fix; a regulatory question handed to Regina
      (or a funding question to Wincent).
   8. **Watchlist**: what could open or close a route, and when to look again.
   9. **Open questions** for the founder.
   10. **Sources**: every `[cN]` and `[wN]`.
   Every fact cited or labelled ("judgement", "unverified"), exactly as in the
   conversation. Drafts (an email, a meeting request) go in an annex only if they were
   agreed in the conversation.
3. **Close the board**: call the `case-board` skill and append one block with
   `"stage": "write_up"` and every section complete (the visuals included).
