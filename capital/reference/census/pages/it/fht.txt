Read more The ancient history of a neural cell population adds clues to brain evolution By studying the developing dentate gyrus, an evolutionarily ancient brain region, Human Technopole researchers found an abundant and diverse population of basal radial glia, essential progenitor cells long associated with neurodevelopment. Conducted with colleagues at the University of Helsinki and Fujita Health University in Japan, the study suggests that these cells may have a much older evolutionary history than previously thought. Read more CellShape-seq: Decoding Glioblastoma through Cell Morphology Researchers at Human Technopole have developed an innovative approach that integrates cell morphology with transcriptional profiling to better classify glioblastoma stem-like cells and predict their behaviour and response to therapy.
