Our Catapult Centres
The Network is comprised of nine Catapults with a national presence spanning over 65 locations.
Cell and Gene Therapy
Located in London, Stevenage, Braintree and Edinburgh
Cell and Gene Therapy Catapult accelerates the translation of early stage research into commercially viable and investible therapies, helping businesses start, grow and confidently develop advanced therapies, delivering them to patients rapidly and effectively. Making the UK a global leader in the development, delivery and commercialisation of cell and gene therapies.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Connected Places
Located in London, Milton Keynes and Birmingham
Connected Places Catapult is the UK’s innovation accelerator for cities, transport, and places. They provide impartial ‘innovation as a service’ to public bodies, businesses, and infrastructure providers to catalyse step-change improvements in the way people live, work and travel.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Digital
Located in London, Belfast and Bristol
Digital Catapult is a deep tech innovation organisation that drives the practical application of deep tech to equip the UK to be future-ready. By partnering with government, industry and academia, Digital Catapult focuses on significant challenges and opportunities facing the UK’s economy and society, where technology can play a major role in providing solutions. The organisation breaks down barriers, de-risks innovation, opens markets and responsibly shapes the products, services and experiences of the future.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Energy Systems
Located in Birmingham
Energy Systems Catapult accelerates the transformation of the UK’s energy systems, identifying priorities and market barriers for decarbonisation. Their vision is to unleash innovation and open new markets to capture the clean growth opportunity, ensuring UK businesses and consumers capture the opportunities of clean growth.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
High Value Manufacturing
Comprised of six Centres including AMRC, CPI, MTC, NCC, NMIS and WMG
High Value Manufacturing Catapult helps grow the UK’s advanced manufacturing value add by helping industry to develop new manufacturing technology. They create the conditions for economic growth by enabling UK manufacturers to achieve significant improvements in their performance and productivity.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Medicines Discovery
Located in Cheshire
Medicines Discovery Catapult is a national facility connecting the UK community to accelerate and reshape innovative drug discovery. Their mission is to industrialise & drive adoption of breakthrough approaches that discover patient-centered medicines, faster improving the productivity and fundability of medicines discovery assets in the UK.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Offshore Renewable Energy
Located in Glasgow, Blyth, Leven, Pembrokeshire and other UK coastal regions
Offshore Renewable Energy Catapult creates clean growth opportunities by accelerating the creation and growth of UK companies in offshore renewable energy. Their mission is to enable the transition to a low carbon economy and expanding the sector by becoming the world’s leading offshore renewables technology centre.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Satellite Applications
Located in Harwell, Wescott, Leicester, Portsmouth and West Cornwall
Satellite Applications Catapult fosters the growth of satellite applications through the exploitation of space. Their vision is to be a world-leading innovation and technology company, helping businesses of all sizes to realise the potential from space. They aim to support integration for a connected and information centric world.
VISIT CATAPULT SITE
Semiconductor
Located in Newport, Bristol and Scotland
Semiconductor Catapult is the UK’s authority on compound semiconductor applications and commercialisation. The Catapult works with start-ups, SMEs, large organisations and academia to de-risk commercialisation, eliminate barriers to market and accelerate compound semiconductor technologies used in transport, future telecoms, energy distribution, quantum and data centres.
