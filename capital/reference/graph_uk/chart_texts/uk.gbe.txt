Our locations
Aberdeen is the perfect location for Britain's publicly owned energy company. It is a world-leading energy hub and has been crucial to our energy security for decades. Now the city plays a vital role in our transition to clean energy.
Hundreds of thousands of engineers, scientists, and technicians call Aberdeen home. That is why we are proud to make Aberdeen our home too. We're committed to becoming a part of the Aberdeen community. We'll draw on the abundant local talent to provide investment and support for projects across the country.
In the longer term, sites in Glasgow and Edinburgh will allow us to maximise access to expertise across Scotland. This combination of regional expertise and national government will make Great British Energy a success.
