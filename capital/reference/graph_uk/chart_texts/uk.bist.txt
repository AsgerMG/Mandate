Our governance - Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade
The main decision-making, executive and managerial bodies at the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST).
The roles and structure of our board and committees.
Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST) Board
The Departmental Board is the principal governance body and is chaired by the Secretary of State. The Permanent Secretary is also a member.
Its remit is to support the Secretary of State in the strategic and operational leadership of the department.

Audit and Risk Assurance Committee (ARAC)
The ARAC is a subcommittee of the board and provides independent advice to support the Departmental Board and Accounting Officer in their responsibilities for issues of risk, control and governance.

Nominations and Governance Committee (NGC)
The NGC is a subcommittee of the board that examines arrangements for:
remuneration, talent management and capability for senior staff
appointments of senior executive and non-executive leaders
effective governance

Executive Committee
The Executive Committee:
drives the department’s overall performance and delivery
ensures the effective management of the department
develops and implements operational strategy
plays a decision-making role on issues escalated from its subcommittees
