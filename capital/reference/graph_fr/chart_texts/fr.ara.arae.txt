Nos atouts
​The Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Entreprises Regional Economic Agency emerged from the Regional Council’s desire to bring together economic services in the region to support businesses, and particularly industrial and industrial services companies.
Présente à travers 11 antennes localesThe Regional Economic Agency guides and supports industrial companies and services to industry businesses at all stages of their development: investment, training and employment, innovation, export, access to funding and European projects, etc.
Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Entreprises is also in charge of promoting the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region throughout the world and championing its multiple assets to attract new businesses.
Since 2017, the Agency has expanded to a team of 150 professionals with recognised expertise, who have supported almost 62,700 businesses. By 2023, the Agency had supported more than 11,000 companies in industry and industry services, with an investment value of over €1.9 billion and a public aid value of over €133 million.

Governance
The Agency governance is composed of the following bodies:
General Assembly: the body representing all members and players in the economic development of the region. It is split into the 7 following colleges :
Companies & financial organisations
Institutions of higher education, research & training
Consular Chambers and CRESS (Regional chamber for social and solidarity economy)
Departments
Metropolises
EPCI (Public establishments for cooperation between local authorities)
Competitive Hubs & Clusters
