Can our funds say enough about themselves to be chosen?

Before anything can name the right fund, the fund has to be legible. This is what production holds today against what it needs, and where the gaps actually are.

Required state, and where production stands

1,021 funds a founder can actually be offered. The notch on each bar is what the target needs.

Says nothing at all (lower is better) 13% → 0%

Carries no description, no eligibility text and no requirement we have cited from its own documents. A founder choosing between these is choosing between blank cards. This must reach zero.

Describes what it funds 59% → 90%

The granting body's own words. This is the single field proven to change a choice: a fund at position 77 of 89 was picked first once its card named what it funds and where.

Carries a requirement we can quote 84% → 90%

A rule traced to the fund's own text. Only these may rule a founder out — the rest can narrow, with their provenance stated.

Says what it typically pays 83% → 90%

Without it a founder cannot tell a voucher from a grant, and the AI cannot rule out a cheque an order of magnitude off.

Says how to apply 24% → 80%

The largest single gap. A fund we name but cannot tell a founder how to approach is a row they cannot act on — the memo names a next step it has no source for.

What this means for the target

An Estonian founder's list is three-quarters blank. A Spanish founder's is not blank at all. Same product, same model, same instructions — one is choosing between funds it can read, the other is guessing. Our accuracy measurements have run on German and French founders, the two best-covered lists in the corpus.

And it is worse in what we print than in what we hold. 13% of funds have nothing; roughly a third of the rows we actually show say nothing, because display rules calibrated for a different problem suppress text we already have. Closing that costs no data and no AI spend.

Measured directly against the production database on Railway — the same store the product reads when a founder asks — not against a local copy. Framework rows and superseded parents are excluded, because a founder is never offered them. 935 funds are searchable by meaning; 1,088 carry a full profile.
