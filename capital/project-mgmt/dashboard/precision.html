What has to be true, step by step — and what we are doing about it

Each step has one condition it must satisfy for the founder to get the right answer. Where it does not, the work in flight is named, with what it unlocks.

  1. 1

    The founder tells us about themselves

    holds

    Must be true: we learn enough in one screen to narrow 1,100 funds to a workable shortlist. It does. No work needed here, and we have measured that adding questions at this stage buys nothing.

  2. 2

    We build the shortlist

    —% holds

    Must be true: the fund they would actually have won is in the shortlist. It is, almost every time. This is the asset — everything downstream is about not squandering it.

    In flight: publishing this week's data improvements to the live database. Why it matters: we improved what the funds say about themselves and the product cannot see a word of it until it is published. Work that is not published has not happened.

  3. 3

    The AI asks what it still needs to know

    built, off

    Must be true: the questions come from looking at these funds, and are ones a founder can actually answer. Built and verified; switched off until the step that uses the answers is switched on.

  4. 4

    The founder answers

    screen live, questions pending

    Must be true: what they type reaches the thing that chooses, and cannot delete a fund by accident. Both now hold — we found and fixed a case where an honest answer silently removed the right fund.

  5. 5

    The AI picks the five

    —% this is where we lose

    Must be true: it can tell the funds apart. Blank cards halved this week — rows saying nothing at all went from 22% to 12%, and in Czechia from 94% to 22%. What is left is not a display problem: those funds hold no description and no eligibility text at all.

    The catch, stated plainly: none of this reaches a founder yet. The cards are read by the AI ranker, and the AI ranker is switched off — the order a founder sees is still our own scoring, which never reads them. The number above cannot move until it is turned on, and it will not be turned on until a measurement says it beats the simple version.

  6. 6

    Five funds and their next steps go into the memo

    —% holds

    Must be true: the founder can act on it. It reads well and we have deliberately not touched it — the memo is not where the accuracy is lost, and spending here would be spending in the wrong place.

The whole argument, in three numbers

We find the right fund —% of the time. We show it to the chooser —% of the time. It reaches the founder —% of the time.

The gap is not intelligence, it is legibility. We are not asking the AI to be cleverer; we are giving it cards it can read. That is why every piece of work in flight is either putting words on a card, or making sure the card reaches the reader.
