Your surface-functionalization work sits inside the electronics and photonics priorities that Horizon EU CL4 funds, and inside the industrial-modernisation push the French state is running through the France 2030 regionalised plan, whose fifth axis, launched by the State and regions on 2026-07-06, is dedicated to modernisation and adoption of advanced technologies by industrial SMEs. Frame your ADEME-cleared project not as research for its own sake but as a production-scale capability: your own stated aim is to move the process to production-ready volumes, which is exactly the axis this new regional plan targets. For the national and regional grant rows, argue on eligible cost and regional industrial impact, the register those schemes actually score, rather than on Horizon-style scientific excellence. Where you do approach Horizon CL4, the register shifts: excellence and cross-border consortium value carry the case, so lead with the technical disruption in your functionalization process rather than production readiness alone.

## State of play

The clearest movement affecting your table is structural, not immediate: Horizon Europe's successor design, the European Competitiveness Fund proposed on 2025-07-16 at €234 billion, would fold Horizon's current clusters into four new windows from 2028, with Digital leadership carrying €51.5 billion and Resilience, security, defence, industry and space carrying €125.2 billion. If you build a consortium case under CL4 now, the thematic framing that wins today may sit under a different window once negotiations with Council and Parliament close, so keep your technical argument portable across both. Nationally, the Chips Act 2.0 proposal reported on 2026-06-30 signals continued attention to Europe's electronics manufacturing base, the same territory your surface-functionalization work sits in, though no new call or gate change has been recorded yet. Your state-aid and regional rows are unaffected by either development for now; they run on national and regional cycles the EU-level reform does not touch.
