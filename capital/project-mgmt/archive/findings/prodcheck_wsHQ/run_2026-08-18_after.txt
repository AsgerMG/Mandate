## Positioning

Frame your pitch around what each door actually rewards. For the Grand Est regional scheme and Bpifrance, the case is industrial: an established manufacturer in Grand Est diversifying a proven hyperfrequency-aerospace process base into a new surface-treatment capability that serves aerospace primes and additive-metal producers. For ADEME, the case is narrower: your written application must land before any experimental work starts, so lead with the project description, not results you do not yet have. For Chips JU and Horizon CL4, the case is consortium fit: your surface-functionalization technology is a process step other value-chain partners need, so position it as a capability a foundry, an additive-manufacturing house or an aerospace tier-1 would want inside their own bid, since your revenue model is equipment sold or leased to whoever eventually bears the cost, the aerospace manufacturer or prime. None of the evidence says you sell to a public buyer or an end consumer, so keep the argument to the process capability and its industrial use, not to any named customer type beyond what you have stated.

## State of play

Horizon CL4 sits inside a live redesign: the Commission's 16 July 2025 proposal folds Horizon Europe's budget into a new European Competitiveness Fund from 2028, split into four windows, with a Digital leadership window worth €51.5B and a Resilience, security, defence, industry and space window worth €125.2B. Which window absorbs industrial process technologies like yours, and on what criteria, is still being negotiated with the Council and Parliament, so an application built on today's CL4 framing may need to be re-argued once the window boundaries are set. EuroHPC has also opened new quantum-access calls and bundled quantum compute allocations into its existing infrastructure rules as of June 2026, which matters if you use HPC resources to validate coatings or deposition processes ahead of an EuroHPC or Horizon bid. Your national and regional rows (Grand Est, ADEME, CNC) are not tracked in this policy corpus, so nothing here implies a near-term change to their gates.
