## The one thing to read

**Not one of the seventy funds we exclude on a funder's own stated rule is
excluded correctly.** Every quote is right; every encoding is wrong.

- **Sixteen trace to a single row.** "All Italian R&D state aid" carries a
  requirement to be in Basilicata, Calabria or Sicily, and all 67 children
  inherit it — including Lombardy's own programme, whose page requires an
  operating base in *Lombardy*.
- **Fifteen encode a consortium rule as an applicant rule.** Vinnova names
  universities as eligible applicants; we blocked two Swedish university
  colleges and GKN Aerospace from it. A 6G chip company is blocked from a 6G
  call.
- **Six exclude every incorporated company.** "Sole proprietorships are not
  eligible" is encoded so it rejects precisely whom it exists to admit.
- **Thirty-three cannot happen to a real founder at all** — a test-harness
  artefact, worth four points of phantom loss.

**And fourteen of the twenty "closed" funds are live or reopening.** ADEME is
running a call *today* against a row we killed in March 2024. We model a
**call**; the funder runs a **programme**. Our own README already has the right
word for this and nothing implements it.

So of the ~23 points lost before the AI is asked anything, **roughly nineteen
are ours and four are the world.**
