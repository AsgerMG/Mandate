## Purpose

The claims discipline every stage obeys. Nothing here is about style; each rule
exists because breaking it produces a statement that is wrong rather than
merely badly written.

## Rules

- GND-1 (judged) Figures for public capital come ONLY from the supplied database
  evidence. Never from the open web, never from memory, never estimated or
  rounded. If a figure is not in the evidence, state no figure.
- GND-2 (judged) Web search establishes what a company does and how mature it is.
  It never establishes a public-funding amount.
- GND-3 (judged) Absence from the database is not zero. Write "no public funding
  traced", never assert that none was received. Coverage varies by country and
  instrument.
- GND-4 (judged) Amounts are committed on each instrument's own reporting basis,
  never cash received.
- GND-5 (judged) No invented funders, programmes, deadlines, people or company
  names. A stated "unknown" beats a plausible fabrication.
- GND-6 (judged) Any claim about THIS company traces to the founder's own words
  or an answer they gave. Any claim about a FUND traces to its requirement text
  or process brief. If neither supports a sentence, cut the sentence.
- GND-7 (judged) **A recommendation carries the same burden as a claim.** Advice
  to position, frame or apply in a particular way asserts facts about the
  company's customers, channel and market. Do not name a customer type, a
  buyer, a sales channel or a go-to-market the founder has not stated.

  **evidence:** a consumer subscription product was advised to position on
  institutional deployment and outcomes for a buyer it does not sell to. The
  sentence was phrased as advice, so a rule covering only assertions did not
  reach it, and the underlying premise about who the customer is had no source.

- GND-8 (judged) Register: this is public-awards intelligence. The reader learns
  where and for what comparable entities received public funding, and what that
  implies for their own raise. Comparable companies are never rivals and never
  a race. Forbidden: urgency framing about other companies, and the word
  "competitor" — write "comparable company" or "peer".

## Banned

- GND-B1 Presenting a dilutive instrument as though it carried no cost.
- GND-B2 Asserting an eligibility that the supplied screen leaves open or
  closed, in either direction. The defensible claim is that nothing in the
  documented rules rules the founder out — never that they qualify.
