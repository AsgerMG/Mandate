# EU Innovation Fund

**Channel code:** `innovation_fund`
**Run by:** European Commission / CINEA, financed by EU Emissions Trading System revenues.
**Portal:** EU Funding & Tenders Portal — https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/programmes/innovation-fund

## What it funds
First-of-a-kind **commercial-scale demonstration** of net-zero and
decarbonisation technologies: renewables, energy storage, hydrogen, carbon
capture/use/storage, low-carbon industry (steel, cement, chemicals), clean
mobility. This is a **conviction-tier** instrument: it behaves like a
state-underwritten growth round, not a research grant.

## Who is eligible
- Public or private entities (single applicants or consortia) with a project in
  an EU member state (plus EEA-EFTA, depending on call).
- Projects must be **innovative** (beyond state of the art), **mature enough**
  to reach financial close in ~4 years, and at meaningful scale.
- Sized by call into large-scale and small/medium-scale lots, plus dedicated
  pilots, auctions (e.g. hydrogen via the European Hydrogen Bank) and cleantech
  manufacturing lots.

## Process and timeline
1. Single-stage full application against an annual call (large topics may use
   pre-qualification).
2. Evaluation on the five award criteria below; ranking.
3. Grant agreement preparation; grants disbursed against milestones, with
   provision for relevant costs over the project life.

**Realistic elapsed time:** roughly a year from call open to grant signature,
then multi-year disbursement. Plan it as a multi-year positioning project.

## Funding rate and ticket
- Grants can cover **up to 60% of the additional (relevant) costs** of the
  project versus a conventional alternative.
- Tickets are large: commonly **tens to hundreds of millions of euros** for
  large-scale projects (median in our corpus is in the tens of millions).

## What evaluators weight
Five criteria: (1) degree of innovation; (2) GHG emission avoidance;
(3) project maturity (technical, financial, operational); (4) scalability /
replicability; (5) cost efficiency (public cost per tonne of CO2 avoided).

## How a founder should act on this
- A comparable company with Innovation Fund money has a state-anchored capex line and a
  credible first-of-a-kind plant — a serious moat. Read it as such.
- This is not a form you fire off: build maturity, secure co-financing, and line
  up the GHG case and offtake early.
- The Clean Industrial Deal is moving support toward exactly this industrial
  capex tier — position as a supplier to, or owner of, the buildout.

## At a glance
*Structured trade-off snapshot. Drives the instrument-comparison table; keep each line to one clause.*
- Ticket: Large capex grants: small-scale from €2.5M, large-scale tens to hundreds of €M
- Speed: 12+ months call-to-grant; milestone-based payout over several years
- You give up: Non-dilutive, but demands matched private capex, a hard GHG-avoidance metric, and a multi-year build commitment
- Best for: First-of-a-kind net-zero industrial and clean-tech capex projects
- Fit: Large-scale industrial (capital-intensive, mid-cap+ tickets)
