# Chips Joint Undertaking (formerly KDT JU)

**Channel code:** `chips_ju`
**Run by:** Chips Joint Undertaking — a partnership of the European Commission,
member states and the industry associations (AENEAS, Inside, EPoSS), under the
EU Chips Act.
**Portal:** https://www.chips-ju.europa.eu and the EU Funding & Tenders Portal.

## What it funds
Electronic components and systems R&D&I across the semiconductor value chain:
design, pilot lines, advanced packaging, materials, and the "Chips for Europe"
initiative. Funds **consortia** against annual calls; co-financed by the EU and
participating states.

## Who is eligible
- Legal entities in EU member states / associated countries, organised into
  cross-border consortia (companies, RTOs, universities, foundries).
- Topics are scoped to the JU's strategic agenda; semiconductor-relevant tech
  is mandatory.

## Process and timeline
1. Open call against the annual Work Programme.
2. Consortium proposal submitted via the portal.
3. Two-tier funding logic: EU grant **plus** national contributions, which means
   you must also clear your **national** authority's process for its share.
4. Evaluation; grant agreement.

**Realistic elapsed time:** several months deadline-to-grant at EU level, with
national co-funding negotiated alongside.

## Funding rate and ticket
- EU grant follows Horizon rates; national authorities top up. Reported amounts
  in our corpus reflect **total public funding (EU + national), not EU-only**,
  so treat the figure as combined public support.

## What evaluators weight
Excellence and impact within the semiconductor agenda; contribution to EU
strategic autonomy in chips; consortium strength across the value chain.

## How a founder should act on this
- A comparable company in the Chips JU is embedded in Europe's semiconductor
  strategic-autonomy push and is co-funded by both the EU and a member state.
- Entry requires a consortium and a fit to the chips value chain; partner with
  RTOs and foundries already inside the JU.
- Confirm your national authority's co-funding route early — the EU grant alone
  does not close the budget.

## At a glance
*Structured trade-off snapshot. Drives the instrument-comparison table; keep each line to one clause.*
- Ticket: Large; EU grant blended with national co-funding
- Speed: 12+ months; call-based
- You give up: National-budget co-funding, a consortium, and a strategic-autonomy framing for your project
- Best for: Semiconductor value-chain R&D and pilot lines
- Fit: Consortium/institutional (3+ partners required; not a default for solo founders)
