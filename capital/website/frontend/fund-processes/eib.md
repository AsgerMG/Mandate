# European Investment Bank Group (EIB / EIF)

**Channel code:** `eib`
**Run by:** European Investment Bank and European Investment Fund.
**Reference:** https://www.eib.org and https://www.eif.org

## What it funds
**Debt and quasi-equity**, not grants: venture debt, growth loans, and
intermediated finance. The EIB lends directly to larger innovative companies and
projects; the EIF supports the venture/growth ecosystem (fund-of-funds, guarantees
to banks and VCs). The **TechEU** programme targets €70bn for tech and innovators
to 2027, aiming to mobilise ~€250bn including direct equity and venture debt.

## Who is eligible
- For direct EIB venture debt / growth finance: typically **scale-ups and larger
  innovative companies** with a fundable plan and often an existing equity round.
- Eligibility is project- and instrument-specific; tickets are larger than
  early-stage grants.

## How it works in practice
- This is a **financing transaction**, not a competitive grant call. You engage
  the EIB/EIF directly or via an advisor; due diligence resembles a lender's.
- Instruments include venture debt (often with a small equity kicker / warrants),
  senior/quasi-equity loans, and InvestEU-backed guarantees delivered through
  intermediaries.

## Ticket size
Larger end of the stack — typically **single to tens of millions of euros** for
direct venture debt / growth loans.

## What they weight
Bankability: a credible plan, repayment capacity, existing equity backing, and
alignment to EU policy goals (innovation, climate, strategic sectors). A strong
public track record reads as state-anchored financing to the EIB.

## Note on our data
EIB amounts in the corpus are recorded as **debt commitments**, not grants —
they are loans/quasi-equity to be repaid, not non-dilutive money.

## How a founder should act on this
- This is a **growth-stage** tool: blend it into the balance sheet once you have
  an equity base and revenue, not as early non-dilutive cash.
- A competitor with EIB financing has institutional debt validation and likely a
  prior equity round — read it as a maturity signal.
- If you are approaching growth stage, the TechEU envelope prices against your
  public track record; a conviction-tier award strengthens the case.
