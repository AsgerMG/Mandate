# National State Aid (EU State Aid Transparency Register)

**Channel code:** `state_aid_satr`
**Source:** EU State Aid Transparency Register (SATR/TAM), aggregating awards
granted by **national and regional authorities** across ~two dozen countries.
**Reference:** https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/competition/transparency/public

## What this channel is
Not a single programme. It is the published record of **aid granted by member
states** under EU state-aid rules — chiefly the General Block Exemption
Regulation (**GBER**), plus notified schemes. Our corpus filters to innovation,
IPCEI and net-zero aid (GBER Art. 21–30 and 36–49, plus notified frameworks) and
**excludes COVID/TCTF emergency aid**. It is the **volume floor** of the public
stack: many awards, local, repeatable.

## Who is eligible
Depends entirely on the underlying national scheme. GBER categories commonly
used: R&D&I aid (Art. 25), aid for SMEs, aid for research infrastructure,
environmental/energy aid, risk-finance aid. Eligibility, rates and ceilings are
set by each scheme and each country.

## How it works in practice
- You do **not** apply to "the register" — you apply to a specific **national or
  regional programme** (a ministry, agency or development bank scheme).
- The award, once granted above a disclosure threshold, is **published** in the
  transparency register. That is what we capture.
- Process, timeline and ticket are scheme-specific; many are faster and more
  local than EU-level grants.

## Ticket size
Wide range; the corpus median for this channel is modest (low hundreds of
thousands), reflecting the many small regional awards. Large notified schemes
(e.g. industrial decarbonisation) sit at the top end.

## Important caveat (Art. 107 blind spot)
Aid granted at **market terms** or below the disclosure threshold, and support
that confers "no advantage," is **not** in the register. Absence here is not
absence of public support — only of *disclosed state aid*.

## How a founder should act on this
- This is usually your **fastest first award** and starts the compounding clock
  cheaply. Find the scheme your country/region actually runs.
- A comparable company with many state-aid awards is working its national machine well;
  identify which national/regional schemes they used (the award titles and the
  granting authority point to it) and check your eligibility for the same ones.
- Your national door depends on your address: development-bank-led (France),
  scheme-led (Germany, Italy), single-agency (UK). Apply to the machine your
  country runs.
