# EIC Accelerator (European Innovation Council)

**Channel code:** `eic`
**Run by:** European Innovation Council / EISMEA (European Commission)
**Portal:** https://eic.ec.europa.eu — applications via the EIC AI Platform

## What it funds
Single-company (mono-beneficiary) breakthrough innovation with market-creating
potential. Two components, usually blended:
- **Grant** up to €2.5M for innovation/development (TRL 5/6 → 8).
- **Equity** (the EIC Fund) €0.5M–€10M for scale-up, taken as a direct stake.
A grant-first and grant-only option exists; "blended finance" combines both.

## Who is eligible
- A for-profit SME or small mid-cap (the equity portion targets startups/SMEs),
  established in an EU member state or Horizon-associated country.
- Technology typically at **TRL 5–6 at entry** (validated in a relevant
  environment), with a credible path to market.
- Not for pure research; the company must be commercialising.

## Process and timeline
1. **Short application** (the "EIC pitch"): a ~5-page summary, slide deck and
   a short video, submitted any time against an open cut-off. AI-assisted form.
2. **Step 1 result** in ~4–6 weeks (pass/fail; remote evaluation).
3. **Step 2 — full application:** full business plan, only if Step 1 passed.
   Submitted against fixed annual cut-off dates (typically 2–3 per year).
4. **Step 3 — jury interview** in Brussels (or remote) for those passing Step 2.
5. Decision, then grant agreement / equity due diligence.

**Realistic elapsed time:** 5–9+ months from full submission to signed grant;
the equity due diligence adds months on top. Treat it as slow money — never
something you bank on for near-term runway.

## What evaluators weight
- **Excellence:** genuine breakthrough / deep-tech edge, not incremental.
- **Impact:** market size, scale-up potential, EU strategic relevance.
- **Implementation:** team, feasibility, and why public money is needed now
  (the counterfactual — what you cannot do without it).

## Odds and realism
Highly competitive; Step-3 success rates are typically single-digit to low
double-digit percent of those who reach interview. A high score that misses
funding can earn a **Seal of Excellence**, which several national schemes pay
out directly — worth chasing as a fallback.

## How a founder should act on this
- If a comparable company holds EIC, they carry a strong third-party certification.
  The academic evidence is that an EIC/SME-Instrument win roughly triples the
  odds of subsequent private investment — match the signal or beat it.
- Enter at TRL 5–6 with a sharp counterfactual. Apply to a national grant in
  parallel so a Seal of Excellence is not wasted.
- If equity is offered, negotiate information rights and exit-consent terms
  before signing; the EIC Fund is a cap-table investor, not just a grantor.
