# Horizon Europe — Pillar II (Global Challenges & Industrial Competitiveness)

**Channel code:** `horizon_eu_pillar_ii`
**Run by:** European Commission (DG RTD and executive agencies, e.g. CINEA, HADEA, REA)
**Portal:** EU Funding & Tenders Portal — https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal

## What it funds
Collaborative R&D and innovation organised into six **Clusters**: Health (CL1);
Culture/Creativity/Society (CL2); Civil Security (CL3); Digital/Industry/Space
(CL4); Climate/Energy/Mobility (CL5); Food/Bioeconomy/Environment (CL6). Funding
flows to **consortia**, not single companies: typically 3+ organisations from 3+
member/associated states.

## Who is eligible
- Any legal entity (company, university, research org, public body) in an EU
  member state or Horizon-associated country.
- A company joins a consortium as coordinator or partner. SMEs are explicitly
  encouraged and often funded at higher rates.
- Topics are prescriptive: you must answer a specific **call topic** in a
  two-year Work Programme, not pitch a free-form idea.

## Process and timeline
1. Find an open **call topic** matching your tech in the current Work Programme.
2. Form or join a **consortium** (partner search via the portal / NCPs).
3. Submit a full proposal by the call **deadline** (single or two-stage).
4. Remote expert evaluation; ranking; Evaluation Summary Report.
5. Grant Agreement Preparation (GAP) for funded proposals.

**Realistic elapsed time:** ~5–8 months from deadline to grant signature
("time-to-grant" target is ~8 months); plus the lead time to build a consortium.

## Funding rate and ticket
- Reimbursement of eligible costs: **100% for research/innovation actions (RIA)**,
  **70% for innovation actions (IA)** for-profits (100% for non-profits).
- A company's slice of a project commonly runs from low hundreds of thousands to
  a few million euros, depending on its work package.

## What evaluators weight
Excellence, Impact, and Quality/Efficiency of Implementation — scored 0–5 each,
with thresholds. Consortium balance, dissemination/exploitation plans, and
alignment to the call's "expected outcomes" matter heavily.

## Note on our data
We load Pillar II at **organisation level**, so company counts here are inflated
relative to single-beneficiary instruments, and consortium roles vary. A Pillar
II appearance means the company was in a funded EU collaborative project.

## How a founder should act on this
- This is a relationship game: get into consortia early, ideally via partners or
  a research institution already inside the cluster.
- Map which cluster your technology serves and track its Work Programme calls.
- A comparable company's Pillar II participation signals EU network embeddedness and
  access to research partners — consider where you could join the same calls.
