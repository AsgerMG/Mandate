# EIT Knowledge & Innovation Communities (EIT KICs)

**Channel code:** `eit`
**Run by:** European Institute of Innovation & Technology (EIT), through its
Knowledge & Innovation Communities: EIT Digital, Climate-KIC, InnoEnergy, Health,
Manufacturing, Urban Mobility, Food, RawMaterials, Culture & Creativity, Water.
**Portal:** https://eit.europa.eu and each KIC's own site.

## What it funds
Innovation, entrepreneurship and scale-up support delivered through the KICs:
acceleration programmes, business creation, grants and sometimes equity/
convertibles via KIC investment vehicles (e.g. EIT InnoEnergy). Sector-specific
to each community.

## Who is eligible
- Startups, scale-ups and innovators in the KIC's thematic area, usually engaging
  through that KIC's programmes or partner network.
- Eligibility and instrument differ per KIC — some are grant/acceleration, some
  take equity or convertibles.

## Process and timeline
1. Apply to a **specific KIC programme** (acceleration cohort, call, or
   investment track), not to "the EIT" generically.
2. Selection by the KIC; onboarding into the programme.
3. Support delivered over the programme cycle (months to multi-year).

**Realistic elapsed time:** programme-dependent; acceleration cohorts run on
fixed intakes, investment tracks on rolling diligence.

## Ticket size
Varies widely by KIC and instrument — from small acceleration grants to
six/seven-figure equity tickets (notably EIT InnoEnergy in clean energy).

## What they weight
Fit to the KIC's thematic mission, team, market traction and the European
innovation-ecosystem contribution.

## Note on overlap
EIT and EIC populations overlap (~12% in our corpus); a company can sit in both.

## How a founder should act on this
- Identify **which KIC** a comparable company engaged (the programme/award names point to
  it) and whether your sector maps to that community.
- KICs are network and ecosystem plays — value is partners, customers and
  follow-on as much as the cash.
- If a KIC vehicle (e.g. InnoEnergy) took equity in a comparable company, treat that KIC
  as an active strategic investor in your space.

## At a glance
*Structured trade-off snapshot. Drives the instrument-comparison table; keep each line to one clause.*
- Ticket: Smaller tickets: typically €50k–€2M via KIC calls, grants and acceleration
- Speed: 3–9 months; rolling KIC calls

- Best for: Startups wanting market access plus grants inside a sector community (climate, health, energy, digital)
