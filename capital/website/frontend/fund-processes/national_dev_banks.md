# National Development Banks & Promotional Agencies

**Channel code:** `national_dev_banks`
**Source:** A composite of national promotional banks and innovation agencies —
e.g. **Bpifrance** and **ADEME** (France), **CDTI** (Spain), **High-Tech
Gründerfonds**, **KfW**, **Bayern Kapital**, **aws** (Germany/Austria),
**Business Finland**, **Vinnova** (Nordics), **CDP** (Italy), **Innovate UK**
(UK), **Innovationsfonden / EIFO** (Denmark), **NCBR / PFR** (Poland), and more.
**Reference:** each institution's own portal.

## What this channel is
The **national industrial-policy layer**: grants, soft loans, guarantees and
sometimes direct equity, delivered by each state's promotional institutions.
This is where most of Europe's company-level public money is actually disbursed,
and it is the **volume product** of the stack — fast-ish, local, repeatable.

## Who is eligible
Institution- and programme-specific, but typically:
- SMEs and innovative companies registered in that country.
- R&D, demonstration, scale-up or green-transition projects.
- Often staged by company maturity (seed grants → growth loans → equity).

## How it works in practice
- Apply to a **specific programme** at the relevant institution (e.g. Bpifrance
  Aide à l'innovation, CDTI's innovation lines via the BDNS, Innovate UK's
  Smart/sector grants, an HTGF seed round).
- Instruments mix: non-dilutive grants, repayable advances, co-investment loans,
  and equity (some of these institutions are also VCs).
- Timelines are generally **shorter than EU-level** (weeks to a few months for
  grants), and decisions are national.

## Ticket size
Broad. Grant lines often run tens to hundreds of thousands; growth loans and
equity reach into the millions. The corpus median for this channel is modest,
reflecting many smaller awards.

## What they weight
Innovation and national economic impact (jobs, regional development, strategic
sectors). Several run as venture investors and weigh team, market and
co-investor syndicate.

## How a founder should act on this
- Identify the institution and **specific programme** behind a comparable company's
  award (the granting body and programme name usually reveal it) and check your
  fit for the same line.
- Use your national door first to start the compounding clock, then stack EU
  instruments on top.
- If the institution invests equity (HTGF, Bpifrance, CDP VC, EIFO, etc.),
  treat it as a cap-table investor: negotiate terms like any VC round.

## At a glance
*Structured trade-off snapshot. Drives the instrument-comparison table; keep each line to one clause.*
- Ticket: Wide: grants and soft loans from €100k to €10M+
- Speed: Fast to medium: weeks to months
- You give up: National-priority alignment; loans are repayable; non-dilutive grants are common
- Best for: The everyday workhorse layer: national grants, soft loans and guarantees in your home market
- Fit: Startup-ready (single applicant, SME-scaled)
