# European Defence Fund (EDF)

**Channel code:** `edf`
**Run by:** European Commission (DG DEFIS).
**Portal:** EU Funding & Tenders Portal — https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu

## What it funds
Collaborative **defence** research and capability development: from defence
research actions (RA) through development actions (DA) — prototypes, systems,
technologies. Funds cross-border **consortia** against annual calls.

## Who is eligible
- Legal entities established in the EU (and Norway, as an associated country),
  not controlled by non-associated third countries (control/IP safeguards apply).
- Consortia of multiple entities from multiple member states; SMEs and
  mid-caps encouraged with bonuses for cross-border SME participation.

## Process and timeline
1. Open call against the annual Work Programme (topic-specific).
2. Consortium proposal via the portal.
3. Evaluation by independent experts on defence-specific criteria.
4. Grant agreement.

**Realistic elapsed time:** several months from deadline to grant.

## Funding rate and ticket
- **Research actions:** up to 100% of eligible costs.
- **Development actions:** co-funded — the EU covers a share (e.g. up to ~20% for
  system prototyping, more for other activities), with member states / industry
  funding the rest. Reported amounts in our corpus reflect **total project cost,
  not the EU-only grant** in many cases; treat the figure cautiously.

## What evaluators weight
Contribution to EU defence capability priorities, innovation, cross-border
cooperation, SME involvement, and operational/strategic relevance.

## Strategic context
EU defence funding is set to **multiply roughly fivefold from 2028**. Today's EDF
writes ~€1bn/year, so current applicants face the thinnest competition the sector
will ever see — an early defence-adjacent track record is a cheap option on the
supercycle.

## How a founder should act on this
- If any part of your stack is dual-use, build the defence track record now,
  before the crowd arrives.
- A comparable company in the EDF holds a defence-prime relationship and consortium
  position; map which capability area they entered.
- Mind the ownership/control rules — non-EU control can block eligibility.

## At a glance

- Ticket: Project totals often €5M–€100M+ across a consortium; the grant is your share
- Speed: 12+ months; annual calls
- You give up: A cross-border defence consortium (3 entities across 3 states), security constraints, and a dual-use or defence remit
- Best for: Dual-use and defence technology developers
- Fit: Consortium/institutional (3+ partners across 3+ states required; not a default for solo founders)
