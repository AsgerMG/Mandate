The dominant co-funding bridge is EIC together with Horizon Europe Pillar II at 444 companies. Pillar II accounts for so much of the corpus that most second-instrument pairings involve it. The next pair is Horizon Europe Pillar II with the national development banks at 158 companies, the strongest evidence in the corpus of national programmes funding the same firms that EU instruments do. EIC together with the Horizon EU Partnerships sits at 68 companies, Pillar II with the Innovation Fund at 55, and EIC with the national development banks at 46.

The widely discussed EIC-to-EIB pipeline remains narrow. Only 10 of the 2,188 EIC-funded companies surface at the EIB venture-debt facility in the data, a flow rate of 0.5 percent. The EU venture-debt facility is not systematically deployed as a follow-on to EIC grants in the period observed. IPCEI continues to show limited overlap with EIC, EDF, or EIB, consistent with its status as a state-aid-notified member-state-led instrument with eligibility rules that differ from European-Commission-run programmes.
