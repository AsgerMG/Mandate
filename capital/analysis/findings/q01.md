<!-- What this shows -->
The EU public-funding corpus covers 3,101 companies across 42 countries, 7 sector labels, and 7 source instrument families, with €54.2B in committed capital and 4,969 recorded events. The EIC dominates by raw event count, accounting for over 2,600 of the 4,969 events, reflecting its status as the EU's broadest-access single-company grant programme. Life sciences (1,090 companies, 35%) and cleantech (941 companies, 30%) together represent two-thirds of the corpus, with deeptech hardware (529, 17%) in third place. France and Germany are the top two countries by company count, but Scandinavian countries punch well above their population weight. Average events per company sit at 1.6 — meaning most companies appear in only a single source family, making the 81 cross-instrument champions an analytically distinct outlier cohort.
