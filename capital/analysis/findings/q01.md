<!-- What this shows -->
The EU public-funding corpus covers 21,689 companies across 53 countries and eight sector labels, with €76.9 billion in committed capital and 58,628 recorded events. Horizon Europe Pillar II dominates: 16,856 of the 21,689 companies are loaded through CL1, CL3, CL4, CL5, and CL6, the largest single instrument in the corpus by a factor of seven. The national development banks of seven member states contribute 2,765 companies, sitting in second place. The European Innovation Council follows with 2,188 companies, the EIT Knowledge and Innovation Communities account for 587, and IPCEI for 301. Cleantech (8,592 companies) and life sciences (4,794) together represent almost two-thirds of the corpus by company count. Spain leads on company count at 3,379, ahead of Germany (2,633), Italy (1,759), and France (1,681). The average company touches the funding machinery 2.7 times.

<!-- Scope qualifiers -->
- The coordinator-only loading convention applies to consortium-heavy instruments (IPCEI, Chips JU, EDF, EIT KICs). The reported company is the consortium lead, not every participant. Company counts for these families are conservative.
- Horizon Europe Pillar II is loaded at organisation level rather than coordinator-only, which is why it dominates the corpus.
- Capital totals are indicative. EDF reports total project cost rather than EU contribution. IPCEI amounts are partially redacted. Innovation Fund H2 Bank amounts are ceiling estimates.
