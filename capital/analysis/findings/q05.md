623 companies (20.1% of the corpus) hold the STEP Seal, but the seal is not distributed evenly across instruments: EIC companies are STEP-flagged at 76%, EDF at 63%, Chips JU at 52%, and Innovation Fund at 40% — while IPCEI (3.6%) and EIT (4.2%) are nearly absent. This pattern is almost entirely explained by the STEP Seal's origins: it was introduced by the European Commission specifically to flag EIC portfolio companies as "strategic" investments, so the EIC overlap is by design rather than discovery. The high EDF rate (63%) is more informative — it indicates that defense-related companies frequently co-appear across the EU's publicly-stated strategic technology list, even though EDF predates the STEP mechanism. By sector, space (46%) and defense (35%) have the highest STEP rates, with digital (5.5%) the lowest — digital software companies are systematically excluded from the EU's "strategic" industrial framing. Country-wise, small EU states (Cyprus, Latvia, Slovenia) show elevated rates that reflect measurement artefact: a country with 16 corpus entries having 7 STEP-tagged creates a 44% rate from small-n noise rather than genuine strategic concentration.
