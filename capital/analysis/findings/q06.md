The time series shows a clear ramp-up pattern: EIC events go from near-zero in 2021 to a peak of 1,073 in 2023, then decline to 842 (2024) and 653 (2025), reflecting both cohort-intake cycles and the fact that 2025 and 2026 entries are still incomplete at extraction time. EDF shows remarkably stable annual event counts (39–63 per year 2022–2026), reflecting the programme's structured call calendar rather than boom-bust dynamics. Innovation Fund accelerates sharply: 6 events in 2021, 29 in 2022, rising to 102 in 2024 — consistent with the fund's expanding budget as ETS revenues grew with carbon prices. Critically, all 317 IPCEI events have NULL dates (no project_started or call_deadline recorded in source data), so IPCEI is entirely absent from the time series — this is a data-collection gap, not a real absence. The sector-mix comparison between 2020–2021 (22 total events) and 2024–2025 (1,879 events) shows the early period is too thin to be informative — the EIC programme only reached scale from 2022 onwards.
