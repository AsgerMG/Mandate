<!-- What this shows -->
Annual event volumes are dominated by Horizon Europe Pillar II from 2022 onward. The 2022 peak of 16,969 events drops to 14,941 in 2023, 15,141 in 2024, and 8,655 in 2025. The 2026 figure of 998 reflects partial coverage at the extraction date rather than a real decline. EIC ramps from near zero in 2021 to a 2023 peak of around 1,000 events and tapers thereafter. EDF posts remarkably stable annual counts between 39 and 63 events from 2022 to 2026, reflecting a structured call calendar rather than boom-bust dynamics. The Innovation Fund accelerates sharply from 6 events in 2021 to 102 in 2024, consistent with an expanding ETS revenue base. The Chips JU and Horizon EU Partnerships sit at smaller stable volumes throughout.

1,554 events have no year, almost entirely the 317 IPCEI events plus 1,237 events from national development bank loads where the source data carried no call deadline or project start date. These are absent from every time-based chart by construction.

<!-- Scope qualifiers -->
- IPCEI: all 317 events have a NULL event year. The IPCEI source data has no machine-readable date field.
- 2025 and 2026 data are partial. Events are loaded up to the extraction date. Year-over-year comparisons beyond 2024 should be treated as incomplete.
- The 1,554 undated events are a real data gap, not a real absence of activity. Most national development bank programmes do publish dates; the gaps are programme-specific extraction issues.
