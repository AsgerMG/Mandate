<!-- What this shows -->
France is the clear dominant country in this corpus (approximately 490 companies), and its concentration is skewed towards defense — a consequence of EDF and IPCEI heavily favouring France's industrial primes. Germany leads in cleantech and is the top country for IPCEI by company count, consistent with its known hydrogen and battery strategies. The Innovation Fund is 100% cleantech by design (confirmed sanity check), and EDF is 90% defense (the remaining 10% are aerospace-classified research organisations like DLR and ONERA, and multi-domain institutes like SINTEF, whose first-appearance sector classification overrides the EDF context). Chips JU reaches only 64% deeptech hardware — not 100% as might be expected — because research universities and multi-domain institutes (CEA, SINTEF, VTT, three Greek universities) hold classifier-inherited sector labels from earlier EIC appearances. Nordic countries (Sweden, Finland, Denmark) cluster strongly in cleantech and life sciences, while Eastern European countries are almost absent from high-value instruments like EDF and IPCEI.
