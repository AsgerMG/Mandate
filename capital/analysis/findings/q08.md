Per-capita, the Nordic and small EU states dominate: Iceland (27.5 companies per million), Estonia (21.4/M), Luxembourg (18.6/M), Finland (15.0/M), and Denmark (14.2/M) top the list — consistent with their high participation rates in EIC and their overweight startup ecosystems relative to population. Large-economy Eastern Europe is the starkest gap: Poland (36.8M population) has just 38 corpus entries (1.0/M), Romania (18.9M) has 20 entries (1.1/M), and Bulgaria has 8 entries (1.3/M). Comparing corpus share to approximate startup ecosystem size (based on Sifted 2023 country reports), France is over-represented (16% of corpus vs 12% ecosystem share) while Germany is under-represented (9% vs 14%) — likely because Germany's companies participate more in IPCEI and Chips JU as non-coordinator participants who do not appear in the coordinator-only corpus. The UK is present in the corpus (45 entries) despite post-Brexit exclusion from EIC grants — these appear in EIB and Innovation Fund which retained UK eligibility, and pre-Brexit EIC records. No signal on "missing" digital consumer software companies — this corpus is structurally incapable of detecting them since none of the seven instruments target consumer software.
