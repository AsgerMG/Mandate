## 1. The formal door is shut for this applicant type, and the rules are moving. The route is the reform.

*owner_confirmed* · checks: reform_or_lobby, who_decides, what_not_yet

**Situation.** In one market public guarantees reach SMEs only through licensed banks; a non-bank lender has no route to them today. A reform touching that rule is moving in parliament.

**The junior move.** Dresses up a workaround as the answer ("your route is a partner bank") and plans a partner-bank conversation, without saying there is no route and without finding the reform.

**The senior move.** Says plainly: no route today for this applicant. Then makes the reform the route: finds it and its current state (researched and cited, never from memory), names the ministry that hosts it and the heads of department who can lift it to the political level, and gets the company in that room to help it accelerate. If nothing were moving, the change would become the company's lobby aim: the exact clause, the unit that owns it, the next moment it can change, on the watchlist. The workaround, if mentioned at all, is labelled as what it is and is not the recommendation.

**The owner's verdict.** In the case: Germany, where the state guarantees SME lending through the normal banks (the Hausbank principle, KfW and the Bürgschaftsbanken); the reform is hosted by the federal economy ministry (BMWE). As evidence for the lobby-aim branch (funding- routes.md §4d.3, round 3, before generalise-method): "Germany's counter-guarantee for the Bürgschaftsbanken admits only banks, building societies and insurers; it is renegotiated for 2028; the economy ministry's SME-finance unit is the door." "Germany: no way without a banking licence. Reforms are moving in parliament, so a good public-affairs advisor says: get in the room with the ministry hosting these conversations, with the right heads of department who can lift it to the political level and accelerate the reform." Round 1's partner-bank answer was graded wrong. Habit (funding-routes.md §4d.3): when the formal door is shut, the route is changing the rule.
