# 03 — Wincent character specification

## Role in Mandate

**Wincent — Public Capital intelligence.**

The website describes Wincent as mapping a company to comparable raises, screened institutions and the priorities shaping grants, loans and public equity.

Wincent's guiding question:

> What can the state put into this company, on what terms, through which instrument, by when, and who administers it?

## Character idea

**The Capital Network.**

Wincent embodies connections between companies, institutions, funding instruments and public priorities. The form should communicate pathways and relationships, not cash or financial speculation.

## Form and silhouette

- Upright, architectural silhouette with a strong central spine or core.
- Interwoven bands, loops or continuous pathways.
- Connected structures that wrap around and pass through one another.
- A sense of movement and possibility, while remaining stable and composed.
- Distinctive from Regina even in silhouette and grayscale.

The form should suggest a mapped network, not a literal coin, bank, currency sign, rocket, trophy or financial chart.

## Material direction

Primary material language:
- Brushed or satin-finished metal.
- Translucent structural glass or ribbed glass.
- A darker internal core or structural element.
- Restrained reflections and verified Mandate accent lighting.

Avoid excessive chrome, mirror finishes, neon blue, gold luxury styling, or a generic “fintech” look.

## Motion language

Wincent's movement is flowing and connective:
- Bands follow a controlled path around the core.
- A subtle light trace travels between connection points.
- Nodes illuminate sequentially to show a route or relationship.
- Pathways connect, pause and resolve into a clear structure.

Motion should communicate mapping and discovery, not money moving, trading, speculation or guaranteed funding.

## Personality through form

- Curious.
- Resourceful.
- Strategic.
- Constructive.
- Forward-looking.

No face, eyes, mouth, hands or human gestures are required. Expressiveness comes from flow, connection, orientation and light.

## Use in content

Good contexts:
- Public funding and capital intelligence.
- Grants, loans and public equity explainers.
- Institutions and funding instruments.
- Comparable raises and funding pathways.
- Wincent's expert profile.

Do not imply that Wincent provides funding, guarantees eligibility, brokers capital or submits applications.
