# 02 — Regina character specification

## Role in Mandate

**Regina — EU Rules intelligence.**

The website describes Regina as reading the official EU, French and German record and producing a source-cited report for a company. Regina's domain includes rules, legislative developments, regulators, timelines and what may need to change.

Regina's guiding question:

> What rules land on this company, when do they bite, and what has to change?

## Character idea

**The Regulatory Structure.**

Regina embodies the organisation and interpretation of complex information. Regina turns multiple layers, dependencies and developments into a coherent picture.

## Form and silhouette

- Upright, composed, architectural silhouette.
- Layered, folded and fan-like structures.
- Clear vertical organisation and deliberate repetition.
- Distinctive stacked or nested planes.
- A stable central axis, with controlled asymmetry.
- Readable as a unique silhouette without relying on colour.

The form should suggest ordered complexity, not a literal book, courthouse, parliament or legal document.

## Material direction

Primary material language:
- Matte architectural ceramic or finely textured mineral composite.
- Layered translucent or satin-frosted glass.
- Restrained internal surfaces or seams in a verified Mandate accent colour.
- Subtle edge lighting to reveal layers.

Avoid making the whole figure look like beige marble, a luxury sculpture, folded paper, or a human wearing a shell.

## Motion language

Regina's movement is measured and precise:
- Layers align or separate slightly to reveal structure.
- A thin light trace travels along a layer or sequence.
- Panels resolve into a clear, ordered arrangement.
- Small nodes illuminate only when communicating a relevant connection.

Motion should communicate examination, organisation and explanation. It must not look like a warning alarm or political campaign animation.

## Personality through form

- Composed.
- Exacting.
- Observant.
- Clear.
- Calmly authoritative.

No face, eyes, mouth, hands or human gestures are required. Expressiveness comes from orientation, spacing, rhythm and light.

## Use in content

Good contexts:
- EU rules and regulatory monitoring.
- Legislative timelines.
- Regulatory developments and source trails.
- “What changed / why it matters” explainers.
- Regina's expert profile.

Do not use Regina as a generic symbol for all Mandate services or as a substitute for legal counsel.
