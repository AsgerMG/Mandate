# 05 — Art direction and generation prompts

Use these prompts as starting points for new concept art. Always attach `assets/selected-visual-reference.png` as the primary visual reference when the image tool supports references.

## Shared style prefix

“Premium architectural 3D character design for Mandate, two faceless non-human living architectural intelligence entities, monumental yet refined, institutional contemporary architecture, elegant vaulted arches, restrained cinematic lighting, tactile high-quality materials, subtle controlled light pathways, timeless and politically neutral, no text, no logos, no flags, no faces, no eyes, no mouths, no human skin, no clothing, no robot parts, no cartoon styling.”

## Regina prompt

“Regina, the EU Rules intelligence entity: a distinctive upright layered architectural form, folded and fan-like planes, ordered vertical rhythm, matte ceramic and mineral composite surfaces with restrained translucent glass layers. The silhouette communicates structure, precision, regulatory complexity and clarity. Use only verified Mandate brand colours as restrained material or light accents; preserve the selected reference's composition and institutional architectural atmosphere.”

## Wincent prompt

“Wincent, the Public Capital intelligence entity: a distinctive upright architectural form built from interwoven brushed-metal bands, connected pathways and ribbed translucent structural glass around a stable central core. The silhouette communicates institutional connections, capital pathways, discovery and opportunity mapping. Use only verified Mandate brand colours as restrained material or light accents; preserve the selected reference's composition and institutional architectural atmosphere.”

## Duo prompt

“Regina and Wincent together as equal partners in one coherent architectural environment. Regina is the layered ceramic / mineral / glass entity; Wincent is the interwoven brushed-metal / glass entity. Equal height, equal visual importance, clearly distinct silhouettes, shared architectural base and visual language. Restrained connection arcs and nodes may link them, but must not resemble a political diagram or financial chart. Use verified Mandate website colours, not an invented palette.”

## Negative prompt / exclusions

“no human faces, no eyes, no mouths, no skin, no realistic people, no suits, no gendered body shapes, no national flags, no EU flag, no party symbols, no religious symbols, no institutional seals, no currency symbols, no money bags, no rockets, no hologram people, no chrome androids, no toy proportions, no cute mascot expression, no excessive gold, no luxury hotel aesthetic, no neon cyberpunk.”

## Production note

Generated images are concept references. For a consistent production system, commission or create a controlled 3D model / repeatable asset set rather than generating unrelated images for every page. Maintain fixed silhouettes, material assignments, camera angles and lighting.
