# 01 — Design principles

## 1. Creative premise

Regina and Wincent are two **living architectural intelligence entities**. They are faceless, non-human, digital presences expressed through sculptural architecture.

They are not human avatars, robots, politicians, mascots, collectible toys, or generic AI assistants.

Their forms should feel:
- Institutional and credible, without imitating a government building or political institution.
- Architectural and sculptural, with a sense of physical presence.
- Digital and intelligent, through controlled light, subtle motion and precise connections.
- Timeless, with no obvious age, ethnicity, nationality, religion or human gender coding.
- Distinct from each other, but clearly part of one visual system.

## 2. The selected visual direction

The owner explicitly selected the image in `assets/selected-visual-reference.png` as the desired vibe.

Preserve these defining qualities:
- Two monumental, faceless, upright sculptural beings.
- Regina: layered, folded, fan-like and structured.
- Wincent: looping, interwoven, connected and flowing.
- A shared grand architectural environment with arches and depth.
- Restrained luminous arcs and nodes connecting the two entities.
- Premium 3D rendering, tactile materials and soft cinematic lighting.
- A shared circular platform or architectural base, used sparingly.

Do not drift into:
- Generic blue hologram people.
- Chrome robots or humanoid androids.
- Luxury-property / museum art direction that overwhelms the product identity.
- Cartoon proportions, cute faces, eyes, mouths or toy-like styling.
- Literal EU flags, national flags, party colours, political insignia or government seals.
- Human clothing, body anatomy or gendered silhouettes.

## 3. Brand relationship

Mandate's existing website is the master brand. Character art must adapt to it, not the other way around.

The characters should feel like a natural extension of the current site:
- Reuse the existing background, typography, colour tokens, radii, spacing and layout conventions.
- Use the site's current accent colours as restrained material or lighting accents only after verifying them in the codebase or browser.
- Do not infer the exact site palette from generated concept images.
- Avoid introducing a new standalone palette that competes with the existing brand.

## 4. Shared visual grammar

| Attribute | Shared rule |
|---|---|
| Form | Abstract architectural humanoid silhouette, no face |
| Presence | Digital / living, communicated through restrained movement |
| Scale | Equal stature and visual importance |
| Lighting | Soft, controlled, premium; no harsh neon |
| Environment | Abstract institutional architecture, not a literal landmark |
| Detail | Deliberate, clean, not over-ornamented |
| Identity | Recognisable in silhouette and grayscale |
| Interaction | Connected, complementary, never hierarchical |

## 5. Political and cultural neutrality

The characters must not imply political affiliation, national identity, religion, ethnicity, ideology or institutional endorsement.

Avoid flags, maps with disputed borders, party-associated symbols, official seals, national costumes, uniforms, recognizable political architecture, and visual stereotypes.

The architecture should be abstract and contemporary. “European” is expressed through the product's subject matter and professional context, not through national stereotypes or political iconography.

## 6. Tone

The visual tone is:
- Serious, not solemn.
- Intelligent, not intimidating.
- Distinctive, not theatrical.
- Warm enough to be approachable, not cute.
- Technological, not sci-fi.
- Institutional, not bureaucratic.

## 7. Non-negotiables

1. No facial features.
2. No realistic human bodies or skin.
3. No national, party, religious or ideological symbolism.
4. Regina and Wincent remain equal partners.
5. Their material distinction must remain legible.
6. Existing Mandate brand tokens take precedence over any new token.
7. Never make the characters imply that Mandate drafts, files, lobbies, guarantees funding, or gives legal advice.

## Not gendered

Regina and Wincent are architectural figures, not people: they are named, never "he" or "she", in copy, prompts and code (owner, 9 Nov 2026).
