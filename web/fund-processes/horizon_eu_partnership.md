# Horizon Europe — European Partnerships

**Channel code:** `horizon_eu_partnership`
**Run by:** European Commission jointly with industry and/or member states,
through dedicated structures (Joint Undertakings, co-funded and co-programmed
partnerships).
**Portal:** EU Funding & Tenders Portal, plus each partnership's own site.

## What it funds
Mission-focused R&D and deployment in areas where the EU pools money with
industry or states: e.g. Clean Hydrogen, Clean Aviation, Key Digital
Technologies / Chips, Europe's Rail, SNS (6G), Global Health EDCTP, Innovative
Health Initiative, Circular Bio-based Europe, and others. Most fund **consortia**
against partnership-specific work programmes.

## Who is eligible
- Legal entities in EU member states / associated countries, as for Horizon
  Pillar II. Many partnerships require or favour membership of an industry
  association behind the partnership.
- Topics are tightly scoped to the partnership's strategic research agenda.

## Process and timeline
1. Identify the relevant partnership and its open calls.
2. Build a consortium aligned to the partnership's roadmap.
3. Submit against the call deadline; expert evaluation; ranking.
4. Grant agreement preparation.

**Realistic elapsed time:** similar to Pillar II, ~5–8 months deadline-to-grant,
plus consortium-building lead time. Some partnerships run regular annual calls.

## Funding rate and ticket
Co-funding rates follow Horizon rules (100% RIA / 70% IA for-profits), but the
**total project cost is often co-financed by industry**, so the EU grant is one
slice of a larger budget. Tickets vary widely by partnership and call.

## What evaluators weight
Excellence, Impact, Implementation — plus fit to the partnership's specific
strategic roadmap and contribution to its KPIs (e.g. tonnes of CO2, deployment
targets).

## How a founder should act on this
- Partnerships reward early, visible presence in the roadmap conversations and
  membership of the backing association.
- A comparable company in a partnership is plugged into a strategic value chain the EU
  is co-funding with industry; identify the partnership and assess whether your
  technology fits its agenda.
- These are multi-year relationships, not one-off applications.

## At a glance
*Structured trade-off snapshot. Drives the instrument-comparison table; keep each line to one clause.*
- Ticket: Typically €0.5M–€5M per partner, partnership-routed
- Speed: 8–12 months from call to grant; fixed annual calls
- You give up: Non-dilutive, but you align to the partnership's strategic agenda and run a consortium
- Best for: R&D that fits a thematic partnership (clean energy, health, key digital tech)
- Fit: Consortium/institutional (partnership-routed; not a default for solo founders)
- Apply: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home
